      How it works

      1. Clean. The text is lowercased and stripped of links, HTML, numbers, punctuation and news-agency datelines.
      2. Weigh words. Each word is weighted by TF-IDF: how often it appears here versus how common it is across all articles.
      3. Score. A Logistic Regression model adds up learned per-word weights to estimate the probability that the article is real.

      Everything runs in your browser: the text you paste is never sent anywhere. The model judges writing style, not facts. It was trained on 2015–2017 political news from one dataset, so treat its answer as a hint and check claims against trusted sources.

      Built with scikit-learn on the Fake and Real News dataset.
